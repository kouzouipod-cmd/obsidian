## AI要約
### **研究の概要**
- 頭頸部腫瘍摘出後の遊離皮弁再建において、頸部郭清術や放射線治療歴が受容血管選択と皮弁不全に与える影響を671症例で解析した後方視的研究。

### **先行研究との差異および優位性**
- 放射線治療のみ、頸部郭清術のみ、両者併用、未治療の4群に分類し、治療歴別の血管選択パターンと皮弁不全の関連を系統的に分析した点で新規性がある。

### **研究手法**
- 2018年4月から2024年3月までの後方視的観察研究。671例を治療歴により4群（RT群82例、ND群43例、NDRT群39例、IN群488例）に分類し、皮弁種類、欠損部位、選択血管、皮弁不全の発生を評価。

### **結果**
- RT群では84本中66本（79%）が照射野内血管、ND群では45本中37本（82%）が郭清野外血管を選択。全体の皮弁不全率に有意差なし。ただし静脈関連不全は放射線治療歴（p<0.05）および照射野内血管使用（p<0.05）で有意に高率。

### **考察および課題**
- 照射野内血管でも成功的使用可能だが、静脈関連不全リスクが高いため慎重な判断が必要。今後は血管壁の組織学的変化や長期成績の検討が課題。

> [!Abstract]

> Background Free flap transfer is an essential technique for head and neck reconstruction after oncological ablative resection. Selection of recipient vessels can be challenging in patients with a history of neck dissection and/or radiotherapy. We analyzed outcomes with regard to recipient vessel selection and flap failure, referring to patients' histories of radiotherapy and/or neck dissection. Methods Records of 671 patients who had undergone head and neck reconstruction in our institution from April 2018 to March 2024 were retrospectively reviewed. We divided patients into four groups based on their treatment history: radiotherapy alone (RT, n = 82), neck dissection alone (ND, n = 43), both radiotherapy and neck dissection (NDRT, n = 39), and intact neck with no history of radiotherapy or neck dissection (IN, n = 488). We collected data on flap types, defect areas, selected recipient vessels, and the occurrence of flap failure. We also assessed the risk factors for flap failure. Results In Group RT, 66 of 84 (79%) recipient vessels were located within irradiated areas. In contrast, in Group ND, 37 of 45 (82%) recipient vessels were outside the dissected areas. No significant association was observed between overall flap failure and variables we assessed. However, our additional flap failure analysis by arterial or venous factors showed an association with the patients' history of radiation treatment (p < 0.05) and in cases where anastomosed vessels were actually located within irradiated areas (p < 0.05). Conclusion In patients with previous radiotherapy history, recipient vessels even within irradiated areas can often be successfully used as recipient vessels. However, after undergoing irradiation treatment, the risk of venous-related flap failure may be higher compared with the non-irradiated area; thus, it necessitates careful consideration during reconstruction.
