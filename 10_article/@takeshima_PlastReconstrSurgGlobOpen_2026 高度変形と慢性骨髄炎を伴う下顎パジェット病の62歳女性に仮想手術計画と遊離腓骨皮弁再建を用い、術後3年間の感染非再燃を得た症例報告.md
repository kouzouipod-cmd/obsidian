## 要約

下顎パジェット病は骨の異常なリモデリングにより高度変形や構造的脆弱性を来し、慢性骨髄炎を合併することがある。通常の解剖学的指標や鏡像再建が使えない症例で、[[バーチャルサージカルプランニング]]を切除範囲と再建形態の決定に用いた単例報告である。

PMC全文によると患者は62歳女性。下顎の慢性感染は局所デブリードマンや保存的治療で改善せず、感染・壊死骨を含めた切除を計画した。病変のない腓骨をドナーとし、顔面幅、上下顎関係、下顎弓の連続性を指標にTruMatch CMF Reconstruction Systemで再建形態を設計した。

下顎区域切除と[[遊離腓骨皮弁]]再建を計画どおりに実施した。手術時間は10時間50分、出血量は1120mL。術後は感染、皮弁壊死、血腫を認めず、3年後CTで移植骨と残存下顎骨の良好な癒合を確認した。追跡中に感染再燃はなく、下顎輪郭と咬合は良好と報告された。

高度変形下の[[下顎再建]]における実行可能性を示すが、対照群のない1例であり、VSPの比較効果を証明しない。定量的な術後形態解析と妥当性が検証された患者報告アウトカムは実施されていない。年齢・3年追跡・手術指標は抄録にない全文補完情報である。

> [!Abstract]

> Paget disease of the mandible is a rare metabolic bone disorder characterized by abnormal bone remodeling, resulting in deformity and structural weakness. Medical therapy, including bisphosphonates, is the most common treatment; however, chronic osteomyelitis is a known complication of mandibular Paget disease, and definitive management with mandibular resection and reconstruction has rarely been reported. Mandibular reconstruction in such cases is particularly challenging because severe deformity obscures normal anatomical landmarks. Virtual surgical planning (VSP) and patient-specific reconstruction systems have been developed to improve surgical accuracy in complex maxillofacial reconstruction. However, their application in Paget disease with severe mandibular deformity has never been described. We report a case of mandibular resection and reconstruction using VSP in a patient with Paget disease of the mandible complicated by chronic osteomyelitis. Due to the absence of reliable anatomical reference, VSP was essential to determine resection margins and reconstruction geometry. Segmental mandibulectomy and free fibular flap reconstruction were performed according to the preoperative plan, resulting in satisfactory postoperative functional and aesthetic outcomes without recurrence of infection. This case suggests the potential utility of VSP for accurate and reliable mandibular reconstruction in Paget disease with severe deformity, where conventional techniques are limited.
