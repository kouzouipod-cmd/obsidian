## 要約

受容血管が限られる既手術頸部で、遊離皮弁が失われた後に介在静脈グラフトを再利用した1例報告。患者は右上口唇・頬骨部の再発扁平上皮癌を有する**77歳女性**で、腫瘍切除後に遊離前腕皮弁で再建された。静脈還流には橈側皮静脈と外頸静脈の尾側断端をつなぐ介在静脈グラフトが用いられた。

初回手術から24時間以内に動脈灌流不全が生じ、術後6日目の再探索で動脈吻合部血栓を確認した。静脈吻合を開く前に、初回皮弁の橈側皮静脈でAcland開存試験を行い逆流を認めた。既存の静脈グラフトを温存し、反対側の遊離前腕皮弁の静脈還流に再使用したところ、再建皮弁は完全生着したと報告されている。

> [!Abstract]
>
> Free tissue transfer in a previously operated neck with limited recipient-vessel availability remains a major reconstructive challenge and may require interposition vein grafts for venous outflow. The fate of these grafts after flap failure is poorly defined. We report a 77-year-old woman with recurrent squamous cell carcinoma of the right upper lip and malar region. Following oncologic resection, reconstruction was performed with a radial forearm free flap. Venous drainage was established using an interposition vein graft between the cephalic vein and the caudal stump of the external jugular vein. Arterial insufficiency developed within 24 hours. Exploration on postoperative day 6 revealed arterial anastomotic thrombosis, while an Acland patency test on the first flap's cephalic vein demonstrated backflow before the venous anastomosis was opened. The graft was retained and reused for venous drainage of a contralateral radial forearm free flap, which remained fully viable. This case demonstrates the technical feasibility and short-term functional success of reusing an interposition vein graft after confirmation of intraoperative patency.
