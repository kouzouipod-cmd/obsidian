## 要約

T4舌扁平上皮癌に対する舌全摘または亜全摘後の再建について、垂直腹直筋皮弁（VRAM）と前外側大腿皮弁（ALT）の機能・腫瘍学的成績を比較した研究。2019年2月–2023年2月の75例を対象とし、**VRAM 30例、ALT 45例**であった。以下は公式PubMed抄録に基づき、全文は未確認である。

皮弁選択は大腿前外側の皮下脂肪厚のpinch testで行い、1.5cm未満をVRAM、1.5cm以上をALTとした。発語・嚥下を妥当性の検証された尺度で評価し、無病生存（DFS）はKaplan–Meier法とCox回帰で解析した。評価時点は方法で「術後1か月と放射線治療終了6か月後」、結果で「術後1・6か月」と表現が異なる。

全体解析ではALT群の発語・嚥下が1か月・6か月評価で良好だった（P<0.05）。一方、type Vの舌全摘サブグループの機能差は1か月のみ有意で、6か月には有意差を認めなかった。2年DFS推定値はVRAM 50.9%、ALT 43.0%（P=0.557）で、皮弁種は独立予後因子ではなかった。

⚠️ 脂肪厚による非無作為の皮弁選択と切除範囲の違いによる交絡に注意する。全体解析と全摘サブグループの結果を分け、非有意差を同等性の証明と解釈しない。低BMI、節外浸潤陽性、浸潤深さ20mm超とDFS不良の関連は示されたが、効果量・信頼区間は抄録に記載されず、T4内のリスク層別化への応用は仮説生成段階である。

> [!Abstract]
>
> OBJECTIVE: To compare functional and oncologic outcomes between vertical rectus abdominis musculocutaneous (VRAM) and anterolateral thigh (ALT) flap reconstruction following total or near-total glossectomy in patients with T4 tongue squamous cell carcinoma.
> 
> METHODS: This comparative study included 75 patients (Group A: VRAM, n=30; Group B: ALT, n=45) treated between February 2019 and February 2023. Flap selection was based on preoperative pinch test of anterolateral thigh subcutaneous fat thickness (<1.5 cm for VRAM, ≥1.5 cm for ALT). Functional outcomes (speech and swallowing) were assessed at 1 month postoperatively and at 6 months after completion of radiotherapy using validated scales. Disease-free survival (DFS) was analyzed using the Kaplan-Meier method and Cox proportional hazards regression.
> 
> RESULTS: Group B demonstrated significantly better speech and swallowing function at 1 month and 6 months postoperatively (p < 0.05). However, in the subgroup analysis of patients with type V (total) glossectomy, functional differences were significant only at 1 month and became comparable by 6 months. The median follow-up was 21.0 months overall and 34.5 months among disease-free patients. The estimated 2-year DFS rates were 50.9% in Group A and 43.0% in Group B (p = 0.557). Multivariate analysis identified lower BMI, positive extranodal extension (ENE), and greater depth of invasion (DOI >20 mm) as independent predictors of worse DFS. Flap type was not an independent prognostic factor.
> 
> CONCLUSIONS: Both VRAM and ALT flaps provide comparable long-term functional outcomes when appropriately oversized. Depth of invasion >20 mm was a strong independent adverse prognostic factor in this cohort of T4 tongue squamous cell carcinoma and was associated with significantly worse disease-free survival. This finding is hypothesis-generating and warrants further investigation as a potential refinement to risk stratification within T4 disease.
