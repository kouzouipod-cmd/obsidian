## 要約

口腔癌患者に対して下顎切除と遊離皮弁による微小血管再建術を行った後の後方気道腔（PAS）体積変化を評価した後方視的単施設研究である。2020〜2025年の間に下顎切除と一次的微小血管再建を受けた42名（女性18名、男性24名、平均年齢66±13歳）が解析対象となった。

術後初回CT検査でのPAS体積は平均11.6%増加（95% CI: −6.8〜29.9%）したが、術後1年の追跡時点（平均381日）では基準値比で平均58.5%の有意な増加を認めた（95% CI: 39.7〜77.2%、P=0.0023）。補助的放射線治療を受けた患者ではPAS増加が有意に大きく（P=0.0266）、複数回帰分析では頸部郭清、欠損型、再建方法、補助化学療法が独立した影響因子として特定された。

これらの結果は、下顎再建がPAS体積を全体として増大させることを示しており、一般的に想定されるような気道狭窄リスクとは異なる知見を提供している。放射線治療がPAS変化をさらに増幅させる機序については今後の検討が必要であり、PASと睡眠時無呼吸症候群や嚥下機能との関連も含めた長期的評価が課題として残る。

> The posterior airway space (PAS) is of considerable clinical relevance, as airway obstruction in this region may compromise patient well-being, manifesting as symptoms ranging from acute dyspnea to chronic obstructive sleep apnea. Although previous studies have demonstrated significant alterations in the PAS following oral and maxillofacial surgical procedures, changes in the PAS among patients with oral cancer undergoing microvascular reconstruction remain insufficiently investigated. This study aimed to evaluate changes in PAS following mandibular resection and microvascular free flap reconstruction, focusing on the impact of radiotherapy. In this retrospective, single-center study, patients with oral cancer treated between January 1, 2020, and January 1, 2025, were assessed. Patients were eligible for inclusion if they had undergone mandibular resection followed by primary microvascular reconstruction and if preoperative and postoperative computed tomography (CT) scans with a maximum slice thickness of 1 mm were available. Imaging data were segmented using 3D Slicer, and PAS volume was quantified in cubic centimeters. Intraindividual changes in PAS volume were subsequently analyzed, and potential influencing factors, such as adjuvant radiotherapy, defect type, and reconstruction method, were evaluated. A total of 42 patients (18 females, 24 males; mean age 66 +/- 13 years) were included in the analysis. Overall, PAS volume increased by a mean of 11.6% (95% CI: -6.8% to 29.9%) on the first postoperative CT scan. At one-year follow-up (mean 381 days), PAS volume had increased significantly by 58.5% (95% CI: 39.7% to 77.2%) compared with baseline (p = 0.0023). The increase in PAS volume was significantly greater in patients who received adjuvant radiotherapy (p = 0.0266). Furthermore, multiple linear regression identified neck dissection, defect type, reconstruction method, and adjuvant chemotherapy as independent factors significantly associated with changes in PAS volume at the one-year follow-up. Mandibular resection followed by microvascular reconstruction is associated with a significant overall increase in PAS volume in patients with oral cancer. However, multiple factors - particularly adjuvant radiotherapy - are significantly associated with the extent of PAS alteration.
