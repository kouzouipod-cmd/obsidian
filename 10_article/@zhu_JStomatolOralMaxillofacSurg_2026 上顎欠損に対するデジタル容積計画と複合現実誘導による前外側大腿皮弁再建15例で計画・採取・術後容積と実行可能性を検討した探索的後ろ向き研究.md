## 要約

上顎再建では欠損の形態だけでなく軟部組織の容積を復元することが難しい。本研究は、CTに基づくデジタル容積計画と複合現実（mixed reality：MR）による前外側大腿皮弁（ALT）採取誘導を組み合わせた手順の実行可能性を検討した。以下はPubMed抄録に基づく要約であり、全文は未確認である。

2023年11月–2025年1月に上顎切除とALT再建を受けた15例を後ろ向きに解析した。Mimics Medical 21.0で仮想骨切り後の欠損容量V1を算出し、ドナー脂肪厚と併せて皮弁を設計した。術中はMRで穿通枝位置と採取を誘導し、水置換法による採取皮弁容積V2と術後CT容積V3を測定した。

V1・V2・V3の中央値は61.57・75.00・64.26mL、IQRは各44.53・52.00・40.65mLだった。全体差は有意で、V2はV1およびV3より大きかったが、V1とV3の差は有意でなかった。皮弁利用率は**91.13±9.67%**、15皮弁すべてが生着し、放射線治療後のチタンメッシュ露出を1例に認めた。

⚠️ 対照群のない小規模な探索研究であり、V1とV3の非有意差は容積の同等性や計画精度の証明ではない。MRや容積計画による臨床転帰改善、トリミング減少の効果は比較研究での検証を要する。発語とQOLは概して良好と記載されるが、尺度の具体的な数値や術後CT評価時期は抄録では分からない。

> [!Abstract]
>
> BACKGROUND: Accurate restoration of soft-tissue volume remains challenging in maxillary reconstruction. This study assessed the feasibility of a defect-oriented digital volumetric planning workflow combined with mixed reality (MR)-guided anterolateral thigh flap (ALTF) harvest.
> 
> METHODS: Patients who underwent maxillectomy followed by ALTF reconstruction between November 2023 and January 2025 were retrospectively reviewed. Preoperative CT data were imported into Mimics Medical 21.0 for three-dimensional reconstruction of the maxilla and tumor. Virtual osteotomy and defect simulation were performed to calculate the simulated recipient-defect capacity, designated as V1. Flap dimensions were then designed using V1 and donor-site adipose thickness. MR guidance was used intraoperatively for perforator localization and flap harvest. Intraoperative flap volume was measured by water displacement (V2), and postoperative flap volume was quantified on CT imaging (V3). Secondary outcomes included flap utilization rate, complications, speech intelligibility, patient satisfaction, and University of Washington Quality of Life scores.The three related volumes were compared using the Friedman test, followed by Bonferroni-adjusted pairwise comparisons.
> 
> RESULTS: Fifteen patients were included. Mean flap thickness was 10.80 ± 3.49 mm. Median V1, V2, and V3 values were 61.57 mL (interquartile range [IQR], 44.53), 75.00 mL (IQR, 52.00), and 64.26 mL (IQR, 40.65), respectively. The overall difference was significant (Friedman statistic = 17.200, degrees of freedom = 2; P < 0.001). V2 was significantly greater than V1 (adjusted P < 0.001) and V3 (adjusted P = 0.003), whereas V1 and V3 did not differ significantly (adjusted P = 1.000). Mean flap utilization rate was 91.13 ± 9.67%. All flaps survived. One patient developed titanium mesh exposure after radiotherapy; no other major complications occurred. Postoperative speech intelligibility and quality-of-life outcomes were generally favorable.
> 
> CONCLUSIONS: Digital volumetric planning combined with MR-guided ALTF harvest was technically feasible in this preliminary cohort and provided a reference for individualized flap design. The findings should be interpreted as exploratory, and comparative studies with larger samples are needed to determine whether this workflow improves clinical outcomes or reduces tissue trimming.
