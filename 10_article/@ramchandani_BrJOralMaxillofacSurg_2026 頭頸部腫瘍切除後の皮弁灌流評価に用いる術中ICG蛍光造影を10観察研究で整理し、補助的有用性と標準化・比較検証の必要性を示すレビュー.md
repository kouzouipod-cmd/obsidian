## 要約

頭頸部腫瘍切除後の遊離・有茎皮弁再建において、術中インドシアニングリーン蛍光造影（ICG-FA）が灌流評価と手術中の判断にどのように関係するかを検討したシステマティックレビュー。以下はPubMed抄録で検証した内容であり、全文は未確認である。

MEDLINE、Embase、CENTRALを開始時から2026年2月19日まで検索し、成人の頭頸部腫瘍再建を対象とした。2名が独立して選択・抽出・バイアス評価を行い、異質性のためナラティブに統合した。採用10件はすべて観察研究で、**463患者・ICG評価468皮弁**を含み、比較研究2件には非ICG対照316皮弁があった。

ICG評価群では再探索56/450（12.4%）、部分壊死4/83（4.8%）、全皮弁喪失15/423（3.5%）だった。血行障害皮弁の救済は33/45（73.3%）、術中方針変更は12/94（12.8%）である。各アウトカムの報告対象が異なり、分母を468皮弁に統一して解釈することはできない。

⚠️ ICG-FAは灌流評価を補う可能性がある一方、これらは主にICG使用群の記述的割合で、対照群に対する効果量を示すものではない。比較研究にも大きなバイアスリスクがあり、標準化されたプロトコルによる前向き比較研究が必要とされる。抄録はルーチン導入を推奨できるほどの根拠には達していないと結論している。

> [!Abstract]
>
> The aim of the current study was to evaluate the clinical utility of intraoperative indocyanine green fluorescence angiography (ICG-FA) for the assessment of perfusion in head and neck oncological flap reconstruction and its association with outcomes and intraoperative decision-making. In a PRISMA-compliant systematic review, MEDLINE, Embase, and CENTRAL databases were searched from inception to 19 February 2026. Eligible studies included adults undergoing head and neck oncological resection with free or pedicled flap reconstruction using intraoperative ICG-FA. Primary outcomes were return to theatre, partial flap necrosis, total flap loss, and flap salvage. Secondary outcomes included fistula, wound dehiscence, infection, and the impact of intraoperative decisions. Two reviewers independently performed the study selection, data extraction, and risk of bias assessment. Owing to heterogeneity, the findings were synthesised narratively. Ten observational studies were included, comprising 468 ICG-assessed flaps in 463 patients; two comparative studies also contributed 316 non-ICG comparator flaps. Most reconstructions were free flaps (90.6%). Re-exploration occurred in 12.4% (56/450) of ICG-assessed flaps, partial flap necrosis in 4.8% (4/83), and total flap loss in 3.5% (15/423). Salvage was achieved in 73.3% (33/45) of compromised flaps. Intraoperative management changed in 12.8% (12/94) of flaps. Risk of bias was significant in both comparative studies and moderate across the non-comparative series. ICG-FA appears to be a useful adjunct to the assessment of intraoperative perfusion, with the clearest potential benefit in flap salvage, flap loss, and operative decision-making. However, the evidence base was limited by the observational study design, heterogeneity, and risk of bias. Prospective comparative studies using standardised protocols are needed before routine adoption can be recommended.
