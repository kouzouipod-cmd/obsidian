## 要約

アルツハイマー病（AD）に対する深頸部リンパ静脈再建は、同じdcLVAという呼称でも接続するリンパ側組織が異なる。Sunらは、**リンパ組織弁―静脈、リンパ節―静脈、集合リンパ管―静脈の3種類**に整理し、圧・流れ・開存性・免疫処理の違いから必要な検証を論じたナラティブレビューを報告した。

ヒトの報告は主に短期・非対照・術式の記載が不十分な研究で、**どの吻合対象もADへの臨床効果や優越性を確立していない**。短期の認知尺度や血液・髄液バイオマーカーの変化は、脳からの排出が増えたことの証明ではない。Nguyenの献体研究も候補となる解剖を示すが、生体の方向性ある流れ、律速部位、治療反応は示さない。

著者は、管腔が明確な集合リンパ管―静脈吻合を機序検証の標準化に最も適した対象とする。ただし**研究設計上の検証しやすさの順位で、治療推奨の順位ではない**。ADの生物学的確認、測定可能な排出経路の異常、流入源と方向を確認した頸部標的、静脈の適合性に加え、3・6・12か月の流れと開存、対照群、盲検化した患者転帰が必要とする。

⚠️ 系統的レビューやメタ解析ではなく、正式なバイアス評価・GRADE評価も行っていない。遅発性の出血・神経症状・感染／免疫関連所見も検討するが、症例報告の時間的関連から発生率や因果関係は決められない。解剖・技術の可能性から臨床効果へ飛躍しないための研究枠組みとして読む。

> Age-related impairment of brain solute clearance has been implicated in Alzheimer's disease (AD), yet procedures described as deep cervical lymphovenous reconstruction do not necessarily target the same lymphatic structure. This review examines glymphatic exchange, meningeal lymphatic drainage, cervical outflow, human lymphatic imaging, lymphatic surgery, early clinical studies, registered trials, and reported harms. Three interfaces can be distinguished: lymphatic flap-vein, lymph node-vein, and collecting lymphatic vessel-vein anastomosis. They differ in the reconstructed structure, pressure-flow conditions, sensitivity to nodal resistance and venous back pressure, likely failure modes, and the extent to which patency can be tested directly. Human evidence remains largely uncontrolled, short term, and incompletely reported. Recent mouse, cadaveric, proteomic, and retrospective clinical reports broaden the evidence base but do not establish a human cervical bottleneck or clinical efficacy. Biomarker changes are hypothesis-generating rather than evidence of enhanced cerebral clearance, and a delayed report of neurological, infectious, and immune events indicates the need for surveillance beyond the perioperative period. Credible evaluation requires biological confirmation of AD, a measurable clearance-axis phenotype, a suitable cervical target, explicit operative reporting, directional target-level flow with durable patency, concordant imaging and biomarker findings, perioperative and long-term neurological and systemic safety assessment, a controlled longitudinal design, and blinded patient-level outcomes. Collecting lymphatic vessel-vein anastomosis is the most anatomically defined construct for standardized mechanistic testing; lymph node-vein anastomosis depends on functional nodal screening and sustained flow; and lymphatic flap-vein anastomosis remains an exploratory mixed interface. This is a research-design ordering based on target definition and testability, not a therapeutic ranking, evidence of efficacy, or evidence of clinical superiority.
